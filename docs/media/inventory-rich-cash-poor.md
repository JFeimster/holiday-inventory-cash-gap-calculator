## Purpose

Show ecommerce and Shopify sellers how supplier payments can precede sales payouts, causing a cash shortfall even during a strong sales period. Direct viewers to model their own cash timing with the free calculator. This video does **not** make loan-approval, funding-speed, or earnings claims.
